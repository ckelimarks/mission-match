## 🎯 PRIMARY TAGLINES

### **Main Brand Line**
> "Trust infrastructure for the agent economy"

**When to use:** Opening pitch, judge introductions, anywhere you need to position what Mission Match IS.

**Why it works:**
- Positions as infrastructure (not just an app)
- "Trust" = the core value prop
- "Agent economy" = future-facing, visionary

---

### **Hero/Hook Line (CURRENT HOMEPAGE)**
> "Your AI knows you. Let it find your people."

**When to use:** Homepage hero, opening hook, anywhere you need instant clarity.

**Why it works:**
- Crystal clear value prop
- Emotional resonance ("knows you")
- Instantly understandable
- Implies discovery and connection

### **Alternative Hero Line**
> "AI archaeologist for your mind"

**When to use:** Demo intro, verbal pitches, anywhere you want to be more memorable/poetic.

**Why it works:**
- Instantly understandable metaphor
- "Archaeologist" = discovery, digging up hidden patterns
- "Your mind" = personal, introspective value
- Memorable and quotable

**Note:** We went with the clearer "Your AI knows you" for the homepage to balance boldness (badge) with clarity (H1).

## 🎬 DEMO SCRIPT LINES

### **Opening (10 seconds)**
"Mission Match is trust infrastructure for the agent economy. Think of it as an AI archaeologist for your mind."

### **ACT 1: Extraction (explaining what's happening)**
"Your AI chat history contains 6 months of decisions, working patterns, collaboration style. Mission Match extracts it in 60 seconds."

### **ACT 2: Provenance (explaining the innovation)**
"But here's what's different: we don't just extract data. We create cryptographic proof of how it was created. This is portable trust."

### **ACT 3: Human API (explaining the vision)**
"Once extracted, your digital twin becomes a queryable API. Others can ask questions, your AI negotiates consent, you control what's shared."

### **Closing (10 seconds)**
"We've built the first two endpoints of the Human API. You own the data. You own the trust."

## ❌ AVOID SAYING

- "AI matchmaking" (too limiting, sounds like dating)
- "Networking app" (commodity, not infrastructure)
- "Profile generator" (misses the trust/provenance angle)
- "Better LinkedIn" (wrong category)
- "Collaboration tool" (we don't facilitate collaboration, we facilitate trust)

---

**Remember:** You're not pitching an app. You're pitching **infrastructure for the future of human-agent collaboration**.
