The Human API Layer

A universal handshake protocol for progressive disclosure of human collaboration context. Not a personality test. Not a hiring tool. Machine-readable collaboration infrastructure for an AI-agent world.

The Problem

Team formation is socially awkward, random, and inefficient. We've all been in that room trying to find collaborators—standing around making small talk, hoping to find someone who works the way we do. No structure. No protocol.

Awkward

Approaching strangers to talk about collaboration feels forced and uncomfortable.

Random

You might miss the perfect collaborator because you didn't happen to talk to them.

Inefficient

Hours of small talk to figure out if you work well together. Trial and error.

The Shift

The world is moving to AI agents. Agents will mediate hiring, partnerships, team formation. Everyone is building agent tools, agent workflows, agent APIs.

But there's a missing infrastructure layer: How do humans exchange collaboration context through agents?

Why This Matters Right Now

People are switching AI providers and losing years of context.

Your AI knows your work patterns, your communication style, your goals. You've spent months training it through conversation.

But that knowledge is trapped. Cancel ChatGPT? Move to Claude? Switch to a local model? You lose everything. No export. No portability. Vendor lock-in.

CPX solves this:

• Extract your collaboration profile from any AI (ChatGPT, Claude, local models)
• Store it in a format you control
• Take it with you when you switch providers
• Use it to find collaborators at events, online, anywhere

We're not asking you to share data you don't already have. We're giving you ownership of data that's currently trapped in vendor silos.

The Vision

CPX is a universal handshake protocol for human collaboration.

Think of it like this: Software exposes endpoints. Humans need to explain their endpoint too. Your work style, your mission, your patterns—structured data that agents can interpret and exchange.

PROTOCOL SPECIFICATION

CPX v0.1

Collaborative Protocol Exchange

A standard for progressive disclosure of human collaboration context. Enables agents to mediate team formation, partnerships, and collaboration in a structured, consent-based way.

Core Concept

CPX enables humans to "explain their endpoint"—exposing structured collaboration context in a machine-readable format that AI agents can interpret, exchange, and match.

Machine-Readable

Structured JSON format that any AI agent can parse and understand.

Progressive Disclosure

Two-stage consent: anonymized overlap first, full context after mutual approval.

Consent-Based

Users control what's shared at each stage. No data revealed without permission.

Protocol Endpoints

Full Profile Access (Requires Consent)

GET /cpx/human/{id}/full

Returns complete profile with PII. Like a Slack bot posting in a channel—requires explicit invitation.

Response when is_consent_granted: false
403 Forbidden • Error: "consent_not_granted"
Hint: Request access via POST /cpx/human/{id}/consent

Grant Consent (Invitation)

POST /cpx/human/{id}/consent

Grant another person's AI access to your full profile. Like /invite @bot in Slack.

Request Body:
{ "handshake_id": "abc123", "duration_days": 30 }

Revoke Consent (Removal)

DELETE /cpx/human/{id}/consent

Revoke access to your full profile. Like /remove @bot in Slack. Takes effect immediately.

Effect: is_consent_granted → false
Other party sees: "Access revoked by user"

Key Principles

1. Structure, Not Scoring

CPX defines how to exchange context, not how to rate humans. No universal compatibility scores. Focus on facilitating conversation.

2. Progressive Disclosure

Share depth gradually. Anonymized patterns first, identified details only after consent.

3. Agent-Mediated

Designed for AI agents to interpret and exchange on behalf of humans. Machine-readable collaboration layer.

4. Extensible

Today: role, mission, work patterns. Tomorrow: calendar data, communication style, creative output. The protocol adapts.

Ethics & Data Sovereignty

CPX is built on Human API principles for ethical data exchange:

Consent

Two-stage, explicit, revocable. You control what's shared at each layer.

Transparency

You see exactly what data is exchanged. No hidden inference.

Autonomy

You decide who gets access, when, and for how long.

Reversibility

Revoke access anytime. Delete your endpoint. Full data portability.

Anti-Extraction

We don't infer without permission. You generate the profile, we don't guess.

Probabilistic Honesty

We show confidence scores. Humans are stateful—this is working documentation.
